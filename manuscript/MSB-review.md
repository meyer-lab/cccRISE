Dear Prof Meyer, 

Thank you for submitting your work to Molecular Systems Biology. We have now received evaluations from two reviewers who agreed to assess your manuscript. As you will see from the reports below, the reviewers agree that the study addresses a conceptually well-motivated question and appreciate the conceptual innovation of the proposed method. However, they also raise a number of significant concerns that would need to be convincingly addressed in a major revision. 

Overall, both reviewers raised important questions regarding the study, including the formulation of the communication score, the lack of rigorous quantitative evaluation and validation, insufficient benchmarking against existing methods, and several methodological aspects requiring further justification. Reviewer #2 also noted that CCC-RISE appears to recover communication programmes that differ substantially from those identified by conventional cell-type-based approaches, raising the question of whether some biologically meaningful communication patterns identified by existing approaches may not be recovered. During our pre-decision cross-commenting, both provided further comments (included below following the full reviewer reports), which should be carefully considered when preparing your revision. 

Addressing the reviewers' concerns in full will be necessary for further consideration, and this appears to require a lot of additional work. I am unsure whether you will be able or willing to address those and return a revised manuscript. On the other hand, given the potential interest of the study, we would be willing to consider a revised manuscript with the understanding that the reviewers' concerns must be fully addressed and that acceptance of the manuscript would entail a second round of review. 

As you probably know, it is Molecular Systems Biology policy to allow a single round of revision only and that, therefore, acceptance or rejection of the manuscript will depend on the completeness of your responses included in the next, final version of the manuscript. For this reason, and to save you from any frustrations in the end we would strongly advise against returning an incomplete revision and would also understand your decision if you choose to rather seek rapid publication elsewhere at this stage. 

Should you find that the requested revisions are not feasible within the constraints outlined here and choose, therefore, to submit your paper elsewhere, we would welcome a message to this effect.
