References
==========

If you use cccRISE in your research, please cite the following papers:

cccRISE Paper
--------------

CCC-RISE resolves cellular communication across conditions at single-cell resolution. Samuel Amidon\*, Ethan Hurt\*, Andrew Ramirez, Nathaniel Thomas, Daniel R. Calabrese, John R. Greenland, Aaron S. Meyer (\*equal contribution). *Under revision at Molecular Systems Biology*, 2026.

RISE Paper
----------

cccRISE is built upon the RISE framework for single-cell analysis across conditions.

Integrative, high-resolution analysis of single-cell gene expression across experimental conditions with PARAFAC2-RISE. *Cell Systems*, 2025. DOI: `10.1016/j.cels.2025.101294 <https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cels.2025.101294>`_

Tensor Decomposition Methods
-----------------------------

cccRISE combines CP (CANDECOMP/PARAFAC) decomposition with PARAFAC2 to analyze the four-dimensional structure of cell-cell communication data. PARAFAC2 allows factor matrices in one mode to change across slices while maintaining a constant cross-product constraint.

Roald, M., Schenker, C., Calhoun, V.D., Adali, T., Bro, R., Cohen, J.E., & Acar, E. (2022). An AO-ADMM approach to constraining PARAFAC2 on all modes. *SIAM Journal on Mathematics of Data Science*, 4(3), 1191-1222. DOI: `10.1137/21M1450033 <https://doi.org/10.1137/21M1450033>`_
